## Overview

Hive Predict is a prediction market built on the Hive blockchain, where users can create and trade on the outcomes of future events. It's built with Aurelia 2 and server-side rendering on a deliberately simple stack, delivering fast page loads and crawlable content alongside a responsive, app-like experience.

## Features

- **Prediction Markets**: Create and trade on the outcomes of real-world events
- **Hive Blockchain**: On-chain settlement and transparency powered by Hive
- **Server-Side Rendering**: Fast first paint and SEO-friendly pages
- **Simple Stack**: A lean architecture that keeps the app fast and maintainable

## Why Aurelia?

Aurelia 2's built-in server-side rendering and clean component model made it easy to keep the stack simple while still delivering pre-rendered, crawlable pages. Its straightforward binding system pairs well with the demands of a real-time market interface backed by blockchain data.
